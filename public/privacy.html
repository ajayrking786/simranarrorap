Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 11, 2026

This policy explains how the Simran Arrora website handles information submitted through accounts, bookings, payments, contact forms, and optional integrations.

Information collected

We may collect your name, email, phone number, date of birth where required for a configured service, booking details, payment reference, messages, and technical session information.

Use and sharing

Information is used to authenticate users, process booking requests, provide support, verify payments, send service notifications, and operate configured Google or payment-provider integrations. Data is shared with a provider only when needed for the requested service or where required by law.

Retention and security

Records are retained only as long as reasonably needed for operations, accounting, dispute handling, or legal obligations. The application uses server-side sessions, password hashing, authorization checks, and environment-held credentials, but no online system can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You may contact the site operator to request correction or ask questions about your information. Contact: configure the public contact email in the server environment before launch.

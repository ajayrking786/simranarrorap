Cancellation and Refunds

Effective date: September 11, 2026

Cancellation

Customers may request cancellation through their account while a booking is pending or approved, subject to administrator review and the configured service terms. The operator may reschedule or cancel a booking when the selected time is unavailable.

Refunds

Submitting payment proof does not guarantee a refund or payment approval. Refund eligibility, timing, and method depend on the service terms and the payment provider. Do not promise a refund unless it has been approved and configured by the business.

How to request help

Send the booking ID, account email, reason, and relevant payment reference through the Contact Us form. Keep provider receipts for review.
